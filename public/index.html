About Game

Miden Sudoku

by danax · Version 1.0
Killer Sudoku with four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. Every puzzle is freshly made and has exactly one solution, so you'll never run out.

Fill each row, column and 3×3 box with 1 to 9, and make every cage add up to its number.

Privacy

Miden Sudoku doesn't collect personal information, and it never asks for your name or email.
